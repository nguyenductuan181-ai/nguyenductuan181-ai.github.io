Workspace Automation Suite

An executive management utility designed to organize personal archives, automate media indexing, and synchronize structured workspace files across Google services.

Purpose and Functionality of Workspace Automation Suite

Workspace Automation Suite is a private personal productivity application developed and operated by Nguyen Duc Tuan (Developer Email: nguyenductuan181@gmail.com). The application is built to eliminate repetitive administrative work by connecting to authorized Google Workspace services through secure OAuth 2.0 protocols.

The core objective of the application is to help the account owner manage documents, organize photos, and generate operational productivity summaries automatically.

Core Application Capabilities

1. Media Indexing & Backup

Accesses personal Google Photos metadata to build structured chronological photo catalogs, verify file integrity, and organize local backup directories without manual sorting.

2. Document & Drive Archival

Automates structured folder management on Google Drive, categorizing incoming operational documents and maintaining historical revision backups.

3. Structured Summary Reporting

Reads and writes operational logs and master indices to Google Sheets and Docs, providing clean dashboards of daily productivity workflows.

4. Status Alerts & Schedule Sync

Sends periodic operational progress reports via Gmail and synchronizes administrative milestone reminders with Google Calendar.

Data Protection and User Privacy

Workspace Automation Suite operates strictly for authorized personal accounts. In compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy:

  • No Commercial Transfer: User data is never sold, shared, or transferred to third-party brokers.
  • No Artificial Intelligence Training: User data is strictly prohibited from being used to train generalized AI/ML models.
  • Owner-Controlled Access: Permissions can be revoked at any moment via Google Account security dashboard.

For complete details on data handling practices, please review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
